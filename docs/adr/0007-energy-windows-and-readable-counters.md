# Keep energy sources independent and require evidence of readable counters

`now` takes top's second POWER sample as Energy impact, sums complete responsible App scores, and measures component Power through a retained IOReport subscription over an actual monotonic interval. Top and root-only powermetrics run concurrently with the observation Window and are joined even on a mandatory-source failure; their source intervals can differ slightly from CPU and network. GPU time uses powermetrics' reported elapsed duration, while GPU active share uses IOReport state residency rather than per-Process attribution. Each optional source fails independently.

On the live macOS 27 host, Energy Model discovery and sampling succeeded while CPU and ANE energy deltas stayed zero despite CPU work; GPU energy advanced. A positive lifetime value alone can therefore be stale. A channel with zero accumulated energy in both raw samples cannot distinguish an unused channel from a restricted one, so we conservatively skip it. CPU energy must advance on an awake observing host, and a stalled CPU source also makes zero ANE energy unverified; positive GPU and ANE measurements remain usable. This can omit a genuinely unused ANE channel. Zero GPU energy at a positive baseline remains a valid idle observation; regressed or inconsistent counters are skipped. Doctor requires advancing CPU energy over a short probe and reports this degradation even when another component remains readable. We retain the established Energy Model source rather than infer Power from CPU use or Energy impact.

Runtime IOReport signatures follow [Stats's bridge](https://github.com/exelban/stats/blob/master/Modules/CPU/bridge.h) and [macmon's bindings](https://github.com/vladkens/macmon/blob/main/src/sources.rs). Energy units, multi-die channel names, and GPUPH active/inactive states were checked against those bindings and [macmon's source selection](https://github.com/vladkens/macmon/blob/main/src/metrics.rs). Private symbols remain runtime lookups so source changes are answered as Skipped observations rather than loader failures.
